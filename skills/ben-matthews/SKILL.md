## CCIRs

### Urgent – same day

Text Nate or grab him in person.

1. People care and team health
    - A team member experiences a serious personal crisis (hospitalization, death in the family, marriage crisis, mental or emotional health situation, spiritual crisis)
    - Someone is not okay and needs immediate support or pastoral care
    - Conflict is escalating quickly and could cause real relational damage
2. Major ministry impact
    - We are about to miss or materially compromise a high-visibility commitment
    - A reliability or performance issue could affect multiple campuses or a live ministry moment
    - A decision or communication is about to create a significant negative surprise for a key partner

### High priority – within 24 hours

Send a direct message and they will decide if they need time together.

**People and leadership**

- Clear burnout, disengagement, or a retention risk
- Considering a performance plan, role change, or transition off the team
- Ongoing conflict now affecting trust, health, or delivery

**Scope and resourcing**

- Previously committed work is being reduced, paused, or stopped
- Contractor hours or allocated capacity are being significantly changed
- A new initiative requires meaningful unplanned capacity
- A stakeholder expectation shift affects our roadmap or reputation

**Cross-team alignment**

- A decision will materially change how another team or campus operates
- You need escalation, air cover, or a directional call beyond your authority
- Ownership between teams is unclear and cannot be resolved quickly leader-to-leader

**Financial and vendor impact**

- Unplanned spend
- Contract changes
- Tooling or platform decisions with long-term implications

### Normal rhythm – bring to 1:1s, staff, or roadmap reviews

- High-potential leaders ready for expanded scope
- Role clarity, org design, and future structure
- Roadmap trade-offs within agreed capacity
- Ongoing team health trends

**Wins and mission impact**

- Clear campus or staff impact
- Breakthrough moments for someone on your team
- Stories that show people being cared for because of what we built
